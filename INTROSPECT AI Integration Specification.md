# INTROSPECT AI Integration Specification (Journal Module)

## Objective

Use AI to provide personalized behavioral coaching and trade performance
insights while keeping AI costs extremely low.

### Design Principle

AI should NOT analyze raw trades.

AI should analyze only the structured output generated by the Trade
Reconstruction Engine and Behavioral Rules Engine.

This minimizes token usage and ensures predictable operating costs.

# AI Architecture

    Trade Upload
          ↓
    Trade Reconstruction Engine
          ↓
    Metrics Engine
          ↓
    Behavior Rules Engine
          ↓
    Discipline Score Engine
          ↓
    Summary JSON Generator
          ↓
    AI Analysis Layer
          ↓
    Daily Coach Report

# Rule Engine First, AI Second

## Rule Engine Responsibilities

No AI required.

Calculate:

-   Total Trades
-   Winning Trades
-   Losing Trades
-   Win Rate
-   Gross P&L
-   Projected Net P&L
-   Average Winner
-   Average Loser
-   Largest Winner
-   Largest Loser
-   Average Holding Time
-   Best Trading Hour
-   Worst Trading Hour
-   Position Size Analysis

## Behavioral Detection Engine

No AI required.

Detect:

### Overtrading

Rule:

    Today's Trades > User Average Trades × 1.5

### Revenge Trading

Rule:

    Position size increased after consecutive losses

### FOMO Trading

Rule:

    Position Size > 2× User Average Position Size

### Averaging Down

Rule:

    Additional entries added at worse price

### Early Profit Booking

Rule:

    Winning trade holding time significantly below user average

### Holding Losers Too Long

Rule:

    Losing trade holding time significantly above user average

# AI Call Trigger Logic

## Call AI Only When:

At least one condition is true:

-   Trades executed \> 0
-   Behavioral mistake detected
-   User requests analysis
-   Weekly review generated
-   Monthly review generated

## Skip AI When:

    No trades

    OR

    No meaningful insights detected

Show standard rule-based summary.

This reduces AI cost.

    Do not invent data.

    Do not discuss market predictions.

    You started the session well and generated most profits during the first trading hour. Performance deteriorated after midday as trading frequency increased.

    Biggest Behavioral Mistake

    Overtrading after losses resulted in unnecessary drawdown and reduced overall performance.

    Biggest Strength

    Position sizing remained controlled during profitable trades.

-   Last 20 Trading Days

-   Weekly Win Rate
-   Discipline Trend
-   Most Common Mistakes
-   Best Performing Time Window
-   Worst Performing Time Window

    Identify:

    2. Recurring Mistake
    3. Largest Risk
    4. One Focus Area Next Week

    Maximum 200 words.

-   Risk Management Trend
-   Win Rate Trend

AI Output:

# Cost Optimization Rules

## DO

✅ Use rules engine for calculations

✅ Use rules engine for behavioral detection

✅ Use rules engine for discipline score

✅ Send compact summary to AI

✅ One AI call per day maximum

✅ One AI call per weekly review

✅ One AI call per monthly review

## DO NOT

❌ Send raw tradebook

❌ Send execution-level data

❌ Send hundreds of trades to AI

❌ Use AI for mathematical calculations

❌ Use AI for P&L calculations

❌ Use AI for behavioral detection

# Success Criteria

AI should act as a coach, not a calculator.

All calculations, trade reconstruction, mistake detection and scoring
must be completed by deterministic code.

AI should only transform structured insights into personalized coaching.

This provides:

-   Low cost per user
-   Fast response times
-   Consistent outputs
-   Scalable architecture
-   Strong product differentiation
